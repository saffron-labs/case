      Questions

      How does buy 3, pay for 2 work?

      Put any three cases in your bag. The lowest-priced one is taken off the total. Six cases means two free, and so on. You can mix designs and phone models.

      Can I try a case before I buy it?

      Yes. Every design on this page is on display at the kiosk. Snap it onto your own phone, check the buttons and the camera cut-out, then decide.

      Is the screen guard really free?

      Yes, one 9H tempered-glass guard with every case, fitted at the counter. If you already have one, we'll give you a cleaning cloth instead.

      Will a clear case turn yellow?

      Our clear cases use anti-yellowing polycarbonate. If one turns yellow within a year, bring it back and we'll swap it.

      What if I change my phone soon after?

      Within 30 days, bring the case back and we'll swap it for the same design in your new model for ₹99.

      How do I pay?

      UPI, cards or cash at the kiosk. Reserving on WhatsApp is free; you pay when you collect.
